COMMON QUESTIONS

Am I a candidate?

The program is appropriate for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher, or 27 or higher with weight-related conditions. We evaluate each person individually.

What happens at the free consult?

You meet a clinician in person, go over your history and goals, and see your exact plan and price before you commit to anything. Benefits, risks, and side effects of any treatment are covered with you there.

Will I regain the weight if I stop?

Weight regain is possible without lifestyle changes. That’s why we focus on nutrition, body composition, and building habits, not just the number on the scale.

What does it cost?
