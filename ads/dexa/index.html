COMMON QUESTIONS

How much does a DEXA scan cost?

$150 for a full body composition scan at Moonshot Medical in Park Ridge, IL.

How long does it take?

About 15 minutes total. The actual scan is approximately 7 minutes while you lie still on the DEXA table.

Is it safe?

Yes. DEXA uses dual-energy X-ray and the exposure is less than a cross-country flight. Non-invasive and painless.

Do I need a referral?

No referral required. Book directly online or by phone.

What does a DEXA scan measure?

Total body fat percentage, lean muscle mass, bone mineral density, visceral fat, and regional body composition (arms, legs, trunk).
