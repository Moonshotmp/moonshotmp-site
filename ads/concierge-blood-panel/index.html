WHY IN-PERSON MATTERS

Accuracy on the markers that move the needle

Venous draws are the clinical standard for hormone panels, advanced lipids, and micronutrients. Finger-prick volumes are too small to run the full panel accurately — vendors fall back to estimates or "qualitative" results on the harder markers.

A clinician who actually reads your results

Apps give you red/yellow/green dots. We sit down with you, walk through every flagged marker, explain why it matters, and tell you what to do about it. You leave with a plan, not a notification.

Follow-up that isn't a chatbot

Numbers off in 3 months? Call us. Need a recheck? We schedule it. Want to optimize a specific marker? We have protocols. The kit ships, the clinician stays.
