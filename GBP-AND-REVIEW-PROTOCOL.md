**Core principle:** Ask at the moment of maximum satisfaction — when the patient sees their results.

### 2.8 What NOT to Do

- **No incentives.** No discounts, gift cards, or credits in exchange for reviews. This violates Google's policies and can get your listing suspended.
- **No review gating.** Don't filter patients (e.g., "only ask happy patients"). Ask everyone at the right moment.
- **No pressure.** One ask, one follow-up message. If they don't do it, move on.
- **No fake reviews.** Never have staff, friends, or family post reviews. Google detects patterns (same IP, same location, new accounts).
- **No bulk timing.** Don't ask 20 patients in one day and then none for 3 weeks. Steady velocity looks organic.
- **No review kiosks.** Don't have patients leave reviews on a clinic iPad. Google flags reviews from the same device/IP.
- **No copying.** Don't give patients exact text to copy-paste. Duplicate language gets flagged.
- **No QR codes on receipts/invoices.** Feels transactional. QR codes should be on standalone cards or wall signage.

### 3.3 Citation Aggregators — Bulk Distribution

Instead of manually submitting to 50+ minor directories, use citation aggregators to push your NAP data to hundreds of directories at once.

**Submit to these four data aggregators:**

| Aggregator | URL | What It Feeds |
|-----------|-----|--------------|
| Data Axle (formerly Infogroup) | dataaxle.com | 70+ directories |
| Neustar / Localeze | neustarlocaleze.biz | 100+ directories |
| Foursquare | business.foursquare.com | Foursquare, Apple Maps, Uber, etc. |
| Factual (now Foursquare) | Merged with Foursquare | Same as above |

**Or use a citation management service:**
- BrightLocal ($29/mo) — monitors NAP consistency across 50+ directories
- Yext ($199/yr+) — pushes NAP to 75+ directories with sync
- Moz Local ($14/mo) — submits to major aggregators

**Recommendation:** BrightLocal is the best ROI. Handles submission, monitoring, and alerts you when NAP inconsistencies appear.
