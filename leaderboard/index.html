SUBMISSION STANDARDS

STRENGTH (1RM)

Full range of motion required. Squat must break parallel. Bench press requires pause at chest. Deadlift requires full hip extension and lockout. Video must show the full rep from a clear angle.

STRICT PULL-UPS

Arms fully extended at bottom (dead hang). Chin clearly over the bar at top. No kipping, butterfly, or swinging. Each rep must be continuous. Video must show full set.

PUSH-UPS IN 2 MINUTES

Chest touches the ground. Arms fully locked out at top. Continuous — any pause longer than 2 seconds ends the count. Timer must be visible or reps counted audibly.

MAX BOX JUMP

Standing jump onto a box/platform. Must land with both feet on top and reach full hip extension. Measure from ground to top of box. Video must show measurement and the jump.

RUNNING EVENTS

GPS watch screenshot showing distance and time, OR video of timed run at a measured track/course. For 40-yard dash, video must show start and finish.

BODY FAT %

DEXA scan report from a certified facility only. No calipers, no bioimpedance, no smart scale readings. Moonshot Medical offers DEXA scans — book yours here.
