Wholesale Partner Terms

Effective: March 2026

1. Overview

These terms govern the wholesale partnership between you ("Partner") and Moonshot Medical and Performance ("Moonshot"). By creating a partner store, you agree to these terms.

2. Pricing Model

  • Retail Price: The price charged to your customers at checkout.
  • Wholesale Price: The price Moonshot charges you for each diagnostic service delivered.
  • Your Margin: Retail price minus wholesale price. This margin is fixed regardless of promotional pricing.
  • If Moonshot runs a promotion that reduces the retail price, the wholesale price is reduced proportionally so your margin remains unchanged.

3. Payment Flow

  • Customers pay the retail price via Stripe Checkout on your connected Stripe account.
  • Moonshot charges your billing card on file the wholesale price after the diagnostic service is delivered.
  • You are responsible for maintaining a valid payment method on file.

4. Stripe Connect

  • You must connect a Stripe account to accept customer payments.
  • All transactions are processed through Stripe and subject to Stripe's terms of service.
  • Moonshot does not hold or process customer payment information directly.

5. Service Delivery

  • All diagnostic services (DEXA scans, blood panels, etc.) are performed by Moonshot's clinical team at our facility.
  • Customers book their own appointments after purchase.
  • Results are reviewed by a licensed medical professional.

6. Partner Responsibilities

  • Accurately represent the services offered through your store.
  • Do not make medical claims beyond what Moonshot authorizes.
  • Maintain current contact and billing information.
  • Respond to customer inquiries related to purchases through your store.

7. Liability

  • Moonshot is responsible for the quality and safety of all diagnostic services performed.
  • Partner is not liable for clinical outcomes or medical decisions.
  • Partner is liable for any misrepresentation of services to customers.

8. Termination

  • Either party may terminate this partnership at any time with 30 days written notice.
  • Upon termination, outstanding wholesale charges must be settled.
  • Moonshot may suspend or terminate a partner store immediately for violations of these terms.

9. Modifications

Moonshot may update these terms with 14 days notice. Continued use of the partner portal constitutes acceptance of updated terms.
