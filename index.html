FAQs

What does Moonshot offer?

Medical optimization and physical rehabilitation under one roof. Medical covers comprehensive blood panels (60+ biomarkers), DEXA body composition scans, men’s and women’s hormone optimization (TRT and BHRT), medically supervised weight loss with GLP-1 medications, and peptide therapy. Rehab covers chiropractic care (McKenzie Method), physical rehabilitation, dry needling, trigger point therapy, and shockwave therapy.

How much does TRT cost?

Men’s hormone optimization (TRT) is $235/month. Women’s hormone optimization (BHRT) is $285/month with the same included services: medical oversight, comprehensive lab work every 6 months, 2 DEXA body composition scans per year, 1 vitamin injection per month, and ongoing dosing adjustments. There are no hidden fees or surprise lab costs.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly for medical services (blood panels, DEXA scans, hormone therapy, weight loss) or rehab services (chiropractic, dry needling, trigger point therapy, shockwave therapy). In Illinois, no referral is required for chiropractic care.
