COMMON QUESTIONS

What services does Moonshot Medical and Performance offer?

Moonshot offers medical optimization and physical rehabilitation under one roof. Medical services include comprehensive blood panels (60+ biomarkers), DEXA body composition scans, men's and women's hormone optimization (TRT and BHRT), medically supervised weight loss with GLP-1 medications (semaglutide and tirzepatide), and peptide therapy. Rehabilitation services include chiropractic care (McKenzie Method), physical rehabilitation, dry needling, trigger point injections, and shockwave therapy.

How much does TRT cost at Moonshot?

Men's hormone optimization (TRT) is $235/month. Women's hormone optimization (BHRT) is $285/month with the same included services. This includes medical oversight, comprehensive lab work every 6 months, 2 DEXA body composition scans per year, 1 vitamin injection per month, and ongoing dosing adjustments. There are no hidden fees or surprise lab costs.

Do I need a referral to come to Moonshot?

No referral is needed for any of our services. You can book directly for medical optimization services (blood panels, DEXA scans, hormone therapy, weight loss) or rehabilitation services (chiropractic, dry needling, trigger point injections, shockwave therapy). In Illinois, no referral is required for chiropractic care.

Where is Moonshot Medical located?

Moonshot Medical and Performance is located at 542 Busse Hwy, Park Ridge, IL 60068. We serve patients throughout the Chicago suburbs and northwest side within approximately a 30-mile radius, including Des Plaines, Niles, Edison Park, Norwood Park, and surrounding areas.
