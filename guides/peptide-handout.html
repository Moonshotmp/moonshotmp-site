Your Biology Has a Clock. It's Already Running.

Growth hormone output drops roughly 14% per decade after age 30 — by 60, you're operating on a fraction of what built you. GHK-Cu, a peptide your body produces naturally to repair tissue and regulate inflammation, falls from approximately 200 ng/mL in your twenties to 80 ng/mL by age 60. Collagen synthesis declines about 1% per year after 25, and tendon healing capacity slows measurably with each passing decade. None of this is hypothetical — it is happening right now, whether you notice it or not.

Peptide therapy does not reverse aging. What it does is restore signaling your body has lost, giving it the instructions to recover faster, build lean tissue more efficiently, sleep deeper, and heal injuries that have stalled. The earlier you intervene, the more biology you have left to work with.

07 Common Questions

Are peptides safe?

The peptides we prescribe are naturally occurring molecules your body already produces — we're supplementing what declines with age. Every prescription is filled by a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy, dosed by a medical provider, and monitored through follow-ups. These compounds have decades of published research.

Why not just buy peptides online?

Testing has found over 30% of gray-market peptides are underdosed, mislabeled, or contaminated. No way to verify what's in the vial, no medical provider adjusting your dose, no recourse if something goes wrong. Our clinic means pharmaceutical-grade compounds, proper dosing, and a provider who knows your bloodwork.

Is this covered by insurance?

No. For context: orthopedic surgery runs $15,000-50,000 before rehab. A 3-month healing protocol runs $635-$955 total — and you keep working and training while you heal.

How long until I see results?

Depends on the peptide. BPC-157/TB-500: 1-3 weeks for pain and recovery. Sermorelin: sleep in 2 weeks, body comp over 6-12 weeks. GHK-Cu: skin by week 4-6. Commit to 3 months — that's what the research supports.

Do I need to be on hormones?

No. Peptides work standalone. They also pair well with hormone optimization for amplified results.

What if I hate needles?

31-gauge subcutaneous needles — same tiny insulin syringes. Into belly fat, not muscle. Most patients say they barely feel it after the first time. We train you in person.

How is this different from HGH?

Sermorelin stimulates YOUR pituitary through natural feedback loops. HGH bypasses that system, shuts down natural production, higher risk, $1,000-3,000+/month. Sermorelin works with your biology.

Can I combine peptides?

Yes — that's what the Wolverine Blend and Glow Stack are. Your provider designs your stack based on goals, labs, and health history.
