01 What Are Peptides?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules in the body. Unlike hormones or pharmaceuticals that override your biology, peptides work with your existing systems — amplifying natural processes like tissue repair, collagen synthesis, growth hormone secretion, and immune modulation.

Think of peptides as precision instructions. Each one targets a specific biological pathway. BPC-157, for example, accelerates healing by upregulating growth factors at the site of injury. Sermorelin stimulates your pituitary gland to produce more of its own growth hormone. These are targeted interventions, not blunt instruments.

Peptide therapy has gained significant traction in performance medicine, regenerative medicine, and anti-aging over the past decade. The research base is growing rapidly, and clinical outcomes are compelling — particularly for healing, recovery, body composition, and sexual health.

At Moonshot, every peptide we prescribe comes from a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy, is administered under medical supervision, and follows an evidence-based dosing protocol. No research chemicals, no gray-market sourcing, no guesswork.

04 503A Compounding Explained

Not all peptides are created equal. The difference between pharmaceutical-grade peptides and "research chemicals" sold online is the difference between a regulated medication and an unverified powder from an overseas lab.

What 503A Means

  • Licensed facility — Compounding pharmacy regulated by the state board of pharmacy and subject to FDA oversight under Section 503A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act
  • Prescription required — A licensed provider must evaluate you and write a prescription specific to your needs
  • Purity and potency testing — Every batch is tested for identity, potency, sterility, and endotoxins
  • USP standards — Manufactured according to United States Pharmacopeia guidelines for sterile compounding

Why this matters: Online "research peptides" have no regulatory oversight, no purity guarantees, and are frequently contaminated, underdosed, or mislabeled. Studies have found that some online peptides contain as little as 30% of the stated dose, and others contain undisclosed compounds. When you're injecting something into your body, sourcing matters.

Every peptide prescribed at Moonshot comes from a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy with full chain-of-custody documentation and third-party purity testing.
