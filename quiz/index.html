Our approach

What these screeners are — and aren't.

Free, validated instruments where they exist. ADAM for low testosterone, MRS for perimenopause, FRAX-aligned inputs for bone density. None of them diagnose anything — they decide whether a clinical evaluation is worth your time.

If a screener flags meaningful eligibility, the next step at Moonshot Medical is a structured intake with a 60-plus marker comprehensive lab panel and an in-person exam in Park Ridge — not a video visit and a prescription mailed from out of state.

These tools do not, and are not intended to, diagnose any medical condition or recommend any specific treatment, drug, dose, or protocol. Screening tools have known false-positive and false-negative rates. Completing a quiz does not establish a provider-patient or treatment relationship, does not constitute a medical examination, and does not entitle you to any specific treatment, prescription, or service. Medical services are provided by Moonshot Medical, PLLC and are available only to patients physically located in states where our clinicians are licensed (currently Illinois).
