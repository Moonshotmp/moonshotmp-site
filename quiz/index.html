What these screeners are

Every screener on this page is free, takes a few minutes, and uses validated symptom instruments where they exist (ADAM for low testosterone, MRS for perimenopause, FRAX-aligned inputs for bone density). None of these tools diagnose anything. They are triage tools designed to decide whether a clinical evaluation is worth your time.

If a screener flags meaningful eligibility factors, the next step at Moonshot Medical is a structured intake visit with a 60-plus marker comprehensive lab panel and an in-person exam at our clinic in Park Ridge, Illinois — not a video visit and a prescription mailed from out of state. Each screener is reviewed by Missy Zammichieli, DNP, APRN, FNP-BC.

These tools do not, and are not intended to, diagnose any medical condition or recommend any specific treatment, drug, dose, or protocol. Screening tools have known false-positive and false-negative rates.
