This combined Privacy Policy and HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices explains what information Moonshot Medical and Performance collects, how we use it, who we share it with, your rights, and what we do if data is ever exposed. 1. Who We Are

"Moonshot Medical and Performance" is a brand operated by two distinct legal entities. The distinction matters because each entity has different obligations under the law and handles different categories of your information.

  • Moonshot Medical, PLLC — the licensed Illinois professional limited liability company that delivers all clinical services (medical evaluation, prescribing, lab interpretation, hormone optimization, peptide therapy, weight loss management, etc.). Moonshot Medical, PLLC is a HIPAA Covered Entity under 45 CFR § 160.103. All Protected Health Information ("PHI") generated through your care as a patient is held by this entity and governed by HIPAA.
  • Moonshot MSO, LLC — the management services organization that operates this website (moonshotmp.com), runs marketing, manages scheduling infrastructure, and provides non-clinical administrative services to Moonshot Medical, PLLC under a Management Services Agreement. The MSO is not a HIPAA Covered Entity. The MSO is, however, subject to the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (16 CFR Part 318) for any "PHR identifiable health information" that flows through the website (for example, quiz responses) before you become a patient.

Where this document refers to "we," "us," or "Moonshot," it refers to both entities together unless we specify otherwise. We disclose this two-entity structure because some of your rights, and the legal regime that applies to your data, depend on whether you are interacting with the website (MSO) or receiving care (PLLC).

2. What Information We Collect

We collect three categories of information. We treat each category differently.

2.1 Clinical-Encounter PHI (held by Moonshot Medical, PLLC)

This is information you provide once you become a patient or are evaluated as a prospective patient by a clinician. It includes:

  • Demographics, contact information, and government identifiers (driver's license, insurance ID where applicable)
  • Medical history, current medications, allergies, family history
  • Vital signs, body composition (DEXA), physical exam findings
  • Lab results, imaging results, and clinician interpretations
  • Prescriptions, treatment plans, clinical notes, and visit summaries
  • Payment information processed for clinical services
  • Clinical communications (secure messaging, SMS appointment reminders, fax records)

All clinical-encounter information is governed by HIPAA and the Illinois statutes referenced in Section 8. It is held in our certified electronic health record system under access controls, encryption at rest and in transit, and audit logging.

2.2 Quiz, Screening, and Pre-Patient Inputs (held by Moonshot MSO, LLC)

When you use a quiz or screening tool on this website (for example, our hormone health quiz, peptide quiz, weight loss quiz, or sleep quiz), you may submit information such as:

  • Symptom selections (e.g., low energy, low libido, brain fog)
  • Screening responses (e.g., self-reported sleep duration, weight, height, body composition goals)
  • Service interest indicators (e.g., interest in TRT, GLP-1, peptides, DEXA scan, NAD+)
  • Email address and, optionally, phone number to receive your results
  • Demographic data you choose to share (age, sex)

Under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, this category is "PHR identifiable health information." It is treated with the same operational rigor as clinical PHI even though it does not yet sit inside the patient medical record. Submitting a quiz does not establish a clinician-patient relationship and is not a substitute for medical evaluation.

2.3 Website Usage Data and Device Signals

When you browse moonshotmp.com we may collect:

  • IP address, approximate geolocation derived from IP, browser user agent, device type, screen size
  • Pages viewed, referring URL, time on page, scroll depth (aggregate analytics only)
  • Cookie and local storage identifiers (only after you grant consent — see Section 7)
  • Search queries you enter into our on-site search
  • Inputs to our AI chatbot (note: do not submit PHI to the chatbot — see Section 6.4)

We do not attach health-condition values to website usage data. The pages you visit on the site are aggregated separately from the answers you give in a quiz, and the two are never joined for analytics, advertising, or third-party transmission.

3. How We Use Information

We use the categories above for the following purposes, and only those purposes:

  • Treatment — to deliver clinical care, write prescriptions, order labs, interpret diagnostics, and coordinate referrals.
  • Payment — to process payment for services rendered (Moonshot Medical operates on a direct-pay model and does not bill insurance directly).
  • Healthcare Operations — quality improvement, internal audits, training, accreditation, and credentialing as permitted under 45 CFR § 164.501.
  • Quiz / Screening Result Delivery — to compute and deliver your personalized results to the email you provided.
  • Marketing — only with separate, affirmative opt-in — we will not enroll you in marketing emails or SMS as a side-effect of submitting a quiz, booking a consult, or becoming a patient. Marketing consent is granular and obtained on a separate checkbox.
  • Website Improvement — using aggregate, non-identifying analytics to improve site speed, content, and usability.
  • Legal Compliance and Risk Management — record retention, regulatory reporting, response to lawful process.

We do not use your health information to target advertising at you. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your health information with social-media advertising platforms (Meta, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest) or with programmatic ad networks.

5. Affirmative Express Authorization (Your Consent)

The FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (16 CFR Part 318) and our own commitment to plain-English consent require us to collect your authorization for any disclosure of your health information to a third party, separately for each recipient and each purpose. We do not bury that authorization in a Terms of Service checkbox, and we do not treat continued use of the site as consent.

Our consent mechanism works as follows:

  • Default-denied. No analytics, advertising, or marketing tag fires until you affirmatively allow it.
  • Granular per-recipient toggles. You can allow Google Analytics while declining advertising pixels, or vice versa. There is no "accept all or accept nothing" option that hides individual recipients.
  • Unbundled from quiz submission. Submitting a quiz does not authorize any disclosure beyond the email needed to send your results. Marketing consent and analytics consent are separate, optional checkboxes.
  • Equal prominence for refusal. The "Decline" button is the same size, color, and styling as "Accept." No dark patterns.
  • Revocable at any time. Visit /privacy/preferences to change any consent. Withdrawal is honored within 24 hours and confirmed in writing.
  • Logged. Every consent grant and revocation is recorded in our consent audit log (timestamp, action, recipient, data categories, IP). You can request a copy of your own consent history.

For HIPAA disclosures that are not for Treatment, Payment, or Healthcare Operations (TPO), Moonshot Medical, PLLC obtains a separate written HIPAA Authorization that meets 45 CFR § 164.508 requirements. Marketing communications, sale of PHI, and most psychotherapy-note disclosures require this separate authorization.

6. Quiz, Screening, and AI Chatbot Tools

6.1 What quizzes are for

Our quizzes are educational screening tools. They help you understand whether a particular service (TRT, GLP-1, peptides, DEXA, etc.) might be relevant to your situation, and they let us send you a personalized informational result. They are not diagnostic tools, they are not a substitute for a medical evaluation by a licensed clinician, and submitting a quiz does not establish a clinician-patient relationship.

6.2 Where quiz data goes

Quiz responses are processed first-party. They are:

  • Transmitted directly to our server — not to any analytics or advertising third party
  • Used to compute your result and email it to you
  • Stored in our database under access controls and encryption
  • Retained for the period specified in Section 10

6.3 What quiz data is not used for

  • It is not used to build advertising audiences.
  • It is not transmitted to Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, X, or any other ad platform.
  • It is not added to your medical record unless you become a patient and your clinician imports it.
  • It is not sold or rented to any third party.

6.4 AI Chatbot

The website chatbot answers general questions about services and pricing using a retrieval-augmented language model. Do not submit PHI, symptoms, or any personal health information through the chatbot. Conversations are logged for quality and safety review for up to 90 days, are not stored as part of any medical record, and are not protected health information under HIPAA. The chatbot does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

7. Cookies, Pixels, and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar storage technologies in three categories. Only the first is on by default.

  • Strictly necessary. Session continuity, CSRF protection, the consent banner itself. These cannot be disabled because the site does not function without them.
  • Analytics. Google Analytics or equivalent — off by default, only loads on non-health pages, only after you affirmatively allow it in the consent banner. IP truncation is enabled.
  • Advertising. Currently disabled across the entire site. If reintroduced, it will be off by default, will not appear on health-topic pages, and will require separate, granular consent.

You can review every cookie that the site may set, and toggle each category, at /privacy/preferences. Your browser's "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal is honored: when received, it is treated as a refusal of analytics and advertising consent.

8. Your Rights

8.1 HIPAA Rights (45 CFR § 164.520, 164.522, 164.524, 164.526, 164.528)

As a patient of Moonshot Medical, PLLC, you have the right to:

  • Receive this Notice of Privacy Practices
  • Inspect and obtain a copy of your medical record (electronic or paper) within 30 days
  • Request an amendment to your record
  • Request restrictions on certain uses and disclosures
  • Request confidential communications (alternate phone, address, email)
  • Receive an accounting of disclosures made other than for TPO
  • Be notified following a breach of unsecured PHI (§ 164.404)
  • File a complaint with us or with HHS Office for Civil Rights (Section 8.4)

8.2 Illinois-Specific Rights

  • Illinois Genetic Information Privacy Act (410 ILCS 513). Genetic test results (where ordered) are not disclosed without your written authorization, are not used in employment or insurance decisions by us, and are subject to heightened access controls.
  • Illinois Mental Health and Developmental Disabilities Confidentiality Act (740 ILCS 110). Mental health records (including any depression, anxiety, or substance use information collected during intake) are subject to a higher consent standard than ordinary PHI. We will not disclose them without specific written consent except as the statute permits.
  • Illinois AIDS Confidentiality Act (410 ILCS 305). HIV-related test orders, results, and treatment information require specific written consent for disclosure beyond the treating clinician.
  • Illinois Personal Information Protection Act (815 ILCS 530). Governs our breach notification obligations to Illinois residents (see Section 12).
  • Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14). We do not collect biometric identifiers (fingerprints, facial geometry, voiceprints) and do not use facial recognition.
  • Illinois Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act (815 ILCS 505). We commit to no dark patterns: no pre-checked consent boxes, no misleading default toggles, no "accept-or-leave" walls, no misleading urgency.

8.3 California Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not provide clinical services to California residents, but California residents may visit this website and submit quizzes. If you are a California resident you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under CPRA, but the opt-out link is provided regardless at /privacy/preferences
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights
  • Designate an authorized agent to exercise these rights on your behalf

8.4 European Union / UK Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

We do not target services to EU/UK residents and our clinical practice is Illinois-only. If you are an EU or UK resident who happens to visit the site, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection under GDPR Articles 15-22. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@moonshotmp.com. Our lawful basis for processing in the limited situations where these rights apply is consent (cookies, marketing) or legitimate interest (security logging).

8.5 How to file a HIPAA complaint

10. Data Retention

We hold each category of information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, plus any retention period required by law. Specific schedules:

  • Medical records (PHI): 10 years after the last patient encounter for adult patients, or until the patient reaches age 28 for minor patients, whichever is later, per Illinois Medical Practice Act and 77 Ill. Adm. Code § 250.1510. Some records are retained longer where required by Controlled Substances Act recordkeeping or insurance defense.
  • Quiz responses (PHR identifiable health information): 12 months from submission, then automatically deleted. If you become a patient and your clinician imports the quiz into your chart, the imported copy follows the medical-record retention rule.
  • Marketing email lists: Held until you unsubscribe; suppressed-but-retained list kept for unsubscribe enforcement only.
  • Cookie / consent records: 24 months for the consent record itself; consent applies session-by-session and is re-prompted on material change to recipients or purposes.
  • Web server logs (IP, user agent, request path): 90 days for security and abuse investigation, then deleted.
  • Aggregate, de-identified analytics: Indefinite. These cannot be re-identified to you.
  • Chatbot conversation logs: 90 days, then deleted.
  • Consent audit log: 7 years (regulatory defense window).

11. Data Security

We implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR Part 164 Subpart C) and industry standards. Specifically:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit for all web traffic and API traffic
  • AES-256 encryption at rest for databases containing PHI
  • Field-level AES-256-GCM encryption for highly sensitive identifiers (driver's license, SSN where collected)
  • Multi-factor authentication for all clinical and administrative staff
  • Role-based access controls and least-privilege principles
  • Audit logging for every read and write of PHI
  • Annual HIPAA risk assessments and penetration testing
  • Workforce HIPAA training at hire and annually
  • Business Associate Agreements with every vendor that touches PHI
  • Incident response plan tested at least annually

No safeguard is perfect. If a breach occurs, Section 12 explains what you can expect from us.

12. Breach Notification Commitment

If we discover that your information has been accessed, acquired, or disclosed without authorization, we will notify you and the appropriate regulators in accordance with the rules below. We treat the discovery date as the date the incident becomes known to any member of our workforce other than the person who caused it.

  • FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (16 CFR § 318.4 - 318.6). For unauthorized acquisition of PHR identifiable health information collected through the website (quizzes, screening tools), we will notify each affected individual without unreasonable delay and in no case later than 60 calendar days after discovery. The notice will include the categories of information disclosed, the recipient(s), the date(s) of disclosure (or best estimate), the steps we are taking to investigate and mitigate, the steps you can take to protect yourself, and our contact information. If 500 or more individuals are affected, we will also notify the FTC and prominent media in the affected jurisdiction within the same 60-day window.
  • HIPAA Breach Notification Rule (45 CFR § 164.404 - 164.408). For breaches of unsecured PHI by Moonshot Medical, PLLC, we will notify each affected individual within 60 calendar days, and HHS within 60 calendar days (or annually for breaches affecting fewer than 500). Breaches affecting more than 500 residents of any state are also reported to prominent media in that state.
  • Illinois Personal Information Protection Act (815 ILCS 530). For breaches affecting Illinois residents, we will notify in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay, and the Illinois Attorney General if more than 500 residents are affected.
  • State analogs. Where a non-Illinois state law requires earlier notification or different content for its residents, we will comply with the more stringent requirement.

Our designated Privacy Officer (Section 15) is responsible for incident triage, regulatory reporting, and individual notification. Our incident-response runbook is exercised at least once per year.

13. Children's Privacy

Moonshot Medical and Performance does not provide services to anyone under 18, and this website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in violation of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501-6506) or its implementing regulation (16 CFR Part 312). If you believe a child under 13 has submitted information through our site, contact privacy@moonshotmp.com and we will delete it immediately.

We will not retroactively apply less-protective terms to information collected under a prior version without your affirmative consent.
