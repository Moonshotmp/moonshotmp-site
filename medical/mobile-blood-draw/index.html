COMMON QUESTIONS

How does the mobile blood draw work?

You promote the event to your members or employees. They sign up and prepay online. We send licensed medical staff to your location on the scheduled date. Patients choose their package, get their blood drawn on-site, and receive results within 3-5 business days with a provider follow-up included.

How many people do we need to make this work?

We're flexible. Whether it's 5 people or 50, we'll make it work. Larger groups just need a bigger time window. We'll help you plan the logistics once you reach out.

What does my business need to provide?

A clean, well-lit space with chairs and a table. That's it. We bring all medical supplies, sharps disposal, and paperwork. A private or semi-private area works best.

Do patients need to come to your clinic at all?

Only if they choose the Elite package (which includes a DEXA scan) or prefer an in-person follow-up. Otherwise, everything can be done at your location and via telehealth — no clinic visit required.

How do I earn the $20 referral fee?

Every patient who books through your mobile event counts. We track bookings by event and pay you $20 per patient after the event is completed. Alternatively, you can pass that $20 as a discount to your members — your call.

Who reviews the results?

Every set of results is reviewed by a board-certified provider at Moonshot Medical. Patients aren't just handed raw numbers — they get a real consultation explaining what their results mean and what (if anything) to do about them.

Do patients need to fast?

We recommend 8-12 hours of fasting for the most accurate results, especially for metabolic and lipid markers. Water and black coffee are fine. That's why morning events work best — people just skip breakfast and show up.
