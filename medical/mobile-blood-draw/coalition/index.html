WHY GET TESTED

Most people train hard and eat right — but have no idea what's happening under the surface.

"Why Am I Always Tired?"

Thyroid dysfunction, low iron, vitamin D deficiency, and blood sugar imbalances are invisible until you test. Most standard physicals don't check.

"Is My Heart Healthy?"

Standard cholesterol panels miss the real risk markers. We test ApoB, Lp(a), and hs-CRP — the biomarkers that predict cardiovascular events.

"Why Can't I Lose Weight?"

Insulin resistance, thyroid issues, and hormone imbalances can stall progress no matter how hard you train. Blood work shows what's working against you.

"Am I Actually Healthy?"

Feeling fine isn't the same as being fine. We test 60+ markers across hormones, metabolism, inflammation, and nutrients — catching problems years early.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I need to fast?

Yes — 8-12 hours of fasting for the most accurate results. Water and black coffee are fine. Skip breakfast and show up.

How long does the blood draw take?

About 10 minutes per person. You'll pick a time slot when you book so there's no waiting around.

When do I get results?

Typically 3-5 business days. You'll get a notification when results are ready, plus a one-on-one follow-up with a licensed provider to walk through everything.

What's the follow-up consultation?

Every package includes a one-on-one with a licensed provider — in-person at our Park Ridge clinic or via telehealth. They'll explain your numbers, flag anything concerning, and discuss next steps. Not a sales pitch — a real health consultation.

Is this covered by insurance?

This is a flat-fee, cash-pay service — no insurance billing, no surprise bills. All packages are HSA/FSA eligible.

What about the DEXA scan (Elite package)?

Blood draw happens at Coalition. The DEXA body composition scan is scheduled separately at Moonshot Medical's Park Ridge clinic (542 Busse Hwy, Park Ridge, IL 60068). We'll coordinate the scheduling.
