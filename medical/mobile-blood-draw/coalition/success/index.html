Fasting Reminder

Accurate results require fasting. Follow these guidelines the night before and morning of the event.

✓

Fast 8-12 hours before your appointment. Stop eating by 10 PM the night before.

✓

Water and black coffee are fine. No cream, sugar, juice, or food.

✓

Avoid intense exercise the morning of the draw. It can skew hormone and metabolic markers.

✓

Wear short sleeves or easy-to-roll sleeves for the blood draw.

✓

Stay hydrated. Drink plenty of water — dehydration makes veins harder to find.
