THE WEIGHT-LOSS PEPTIDE FAMILY

The peptides that actually drive weight loss in clinical practice are GLP-1 receptor agonists. Each has a different profile.

Semaglutide

Single-receptor GLP-1 agonist. Phase 3 trial average ~15% body weight loss at 68 weeks. Brand names Ozempic and Wegovy. Available compounded through licensed 503A/503B pharmacies. Longer real-world track record than newer agents.

Tirzepatide

Dual GLP-1 + GIP receptor agonist. Tends to produce greater average weight loss in head-to-head trials (~20–22% at high dose). Brand names Mounjaro and Zepbound. Different tolerability profile — some patients tolerate it better than semaglutide, some worse.

What about the next-generation peptides?

Newer triple-agonist GLP-1/GIP/glucagon peptides are in trials but are not currently FDA-approved and are not eligible for compounding. We follow the data and update our formulary as the regulatory and clinical landscape changes.

Other "weight loss peptides" online

A lot of marketing labels other peptides as "weight loss peptides" — most of these have no meaningful clinical evidence for fat loss in humans. We focus on what the trials and the real-world data actually support.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Are GLP-1 medications actually peptides?

Yes. Semaglutide and tirzepatide are peptide-based molecules — chains of amino acids modified for stability and receptor binding. They are the most clinically validated weight-loss peptides currently available.

Semaglutide vs tirzepatide — which is better?

Tirzepatide tends to produce greater average weight loss; semaglutide has a longer real-world track record. Tolerability differs by patient. We discuss both at intake. Read the comparison.

Will I regain the weight if I stop?

Many patients regain partially. Sustainability depends on what your habits and body composition look like when you discontinue. Patients who built lean mass during treatment and learned a sustainable eating pattern hold weight loss better than those who only lost weight.

What about other "weight loss peptides" I see online?

A lot of products labeled as "weight loss peptides" online have no clinical evidence behind them for fat loss in humans. We prescribe what the trials and real-world data support — currently the GLP-1 family. If a new molecule earns the evidence, we'll evaluate it.
