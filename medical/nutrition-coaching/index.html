Lasting change does not come from a rigid plan you push through for a few weeks. It comes from small, sustainable shifts, built one at a time, with someone in your corner who adjusts the plan as your life changes.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I have to be a Moonshot patient?

No. Nutrition coaching is open to anyone. Many clients start after a DEXA scan or while on a GLP-1 or hormone program, but no other Moonshot service is required.

In person or virtual?

Both. The Jumpstart session, Foundations, and Partnership+ can be in person at 542 Busse Hwy in Park Ridge or virtual. All-In is virtual only.

What happens in the Jumpstart session?

A single 45-minute working session. Sarah reviews your current diet, grocery list, and meal plan, identifies the highest-leverage changes, looks at where your macros are coming from, and shows how exercise fits alongside your nutrition. No ongoing commitment.

Does it work alongside GLP-1 weight loss?

Yes. GLP-1 medications reduce appetite, which makes protein intake and lean-mass preservation the main nutrition challenge. Coaching focuses on hitting protein targets, building sustainable habits, and tracking body composition with DEXA so the weight you lose is fat, not muscle.

Can I change tiers later?

Yes. Foundations is month to month with no minimum. Partnership+ and All-In carry a 3-month minimum and are billed monthly. You can move between tiers as your needs change.
