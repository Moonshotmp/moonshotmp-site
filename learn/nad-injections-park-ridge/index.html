WHAT IS NAD+ AND WHY DOES IT MATTER?

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every living cell. It is not a vitamin, not a supplement trend, and not optional — it is required for the most fundamental metabolic processes that keep you alive.

NAD+ drives three critical functions: mitochondrial energy production (converting food into ATP, the energy currency your cells run on), DNA repair (PARP enzymes that fix tens of thousands of DNA lesions per day require NAD+ to function), and sirtuin activation (the "longevity genes" that regulate inflammation, stress resistance, and cellular repair are NAD+-dependent enzymes).

Here is the problem: NAD+ levels decline approximately 50% by age 60. This decline correlates directly with reduced energy, slower recovery, impaired cognitive function, and the metabolic dysfunction that characterizes aging. Oral NAD+ supplements have poor bioavailability — most is broken down in the gut before reaching your cells. Injectable NAD+ bypasses digestion entirely, delivering the full dose to the bloodstream.
