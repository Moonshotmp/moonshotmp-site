WHY MEN IN PARK RIDGE ARE CHOOSING IN-PERSON TRT

Telehealth TRT clinics are everywhere. They'll prescribe testosterone from a 10-minute video call and basic labs. Here's why that's not good enough -- especially for men over 40 navigating real testosterone decline.

The Telehealth Problem

Most online TRT clinics test 10-15 basic markers, prescribe a standard dose, and check in every few months. They don't track body composition. They don't look at thyroid, metabolic health, or cardiovascular risk markers. You get a prescription, not a protocol.

The result: You might feel better temporarily, but you have no idea what's actually happening inside your body.

The Moonshot Approach

We test 60+ biomarkers before prescribing anything. We use DEXA scans to track lean mass, fat mass, and bone density over time. We see you in person. We adjust protocols based on data, not guesswork.

The result: You know exactly what's changing, why it's changing, and whether your protocol is actually working.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Is TRT the same as steroids?

No. TRT restores testosterone to normal, healthy levels under medical supervision. It's a completely different thing from anabolic steroid abuse, which involves supraphysiologic doses without monitoring. Read our full comparison.

How do I know if I need TRT?

Symptoms like persistent fatigue, low libido, brain fog, muscle loss, and mood changes can indicate low testosterone. But you need blood work to know for sure. We test 60+ markers to get the full picture—not just total testosterone. Review the full symptom list.

Why not just use an online TRT clinic?

Online clinics prescribe based on minimal testing and video calls. They don't track body composition, don't test 60+ biomarkers, and don't see you in person. You deserve more than a prescription—you need a protocol with real oversight and measurable outcomes.

How quickly will I feel results?

Most men notice improved energy and mood within 2-4 weeks. Libido changes typically follow at 3-6 weeks. Body composition changes (muscle gain, fat loss) become measurable on DEXA at 3-6 months. Full optimization often takes 6-12 months of protocol refinement. Read the full week-by-week TRT timeline.

Do you accept insurance?

We're a direct-pay clinic, which means no insurance middleman dictating what we can test or treat. This lets us run comprehensive panels and design protocols based on what's optimal for you—not what an insurance company approves. Many patients use HSA/FSA funds.
