WHAT IS THE WOLVERINE BLEND?

The Wolverine Blend is a combination peptide therapy that pairs BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) and TB-500 (a fragment of thymosin beta-4) in a single vial. Instead of drawing from two separate vials and giving yourself two injections, you get both peptides in one shot.

The name comes from the optimization medicine community — a reference to the Marvel character Wolverine's regenerative abilities. It's a nickname, not a brand. The concept is straightforward: combine the two most studied healing peptides at an optimal ratio so they work together.

Each 3ml vial contains approximately 1.66mg/ml of BPC-157 and 3.33mg/ml of TB-500. That works out to roughly 5mg BPC-157 and 10mg TB-500 per vial — pre-mixed by a licensed compounding pharmacy.

Key point: This isn't two random peptides thrown together. BPC-157 and TB-500 target tissue repair through genuinely different mechanisms — they complement each other rather than duplicate the same pathway.

WHY COMBINE BPC-157 AND TB-500?

The logic isn't "more peptides = better." It's that these two peptides address different bottlenecks in the healing process. BPC-157 creates the conditions for repair. TB-500 mobilizes the cells that do the repairing.

BPC-157 Mechanism

  • Growth factor upregulation — increases expression of VEGF, FGF, and other growth factors at injury sites
  • Angiogenesis — promotes formation of new blood vessels, improving nutrient and oxygen delivery to damaged tissue
  • Nitric oxide modulation — regulates blood flow and inflammation through the NO system
  • Gut-brain axis — derived from gastric juice, with particular affinity for GI tissue repair

TB-500 Mechanism

  • Actin upregulation — increases production of actin, a key protein for cell structure, migration, and repair
  • Cell migration — promotes movement of repair cells (fibroblasts, endothelial cells) to the injury site
  • Anti-inflammatory — reduces inflammatory cytokines and dampens excessive immune response
  • Systemic reach — low molecular weight allows it to travel through tissues more easily than most peptides

Combined Effect

  • Multi-pathway repair — addresses both the infrastructure (blood supply, growth factors) and the workforce (cell migration, tissue building)
  • Broader tissue coverage — BPC-157's gut and tendon affinity pairs with TB-500's muscle and joint effects
  • Faster onset — simultaneous action on different repair bottlenecks may accelerate overall healing timeline
  • Reduced inflammation — both peptides modulate inflammation through different pathways

This isn't theoretical stacking — the mechanisms are genuinely complementary. BPC-157 creates the conditions for healing (blood vessels, growth factors), and TB-500 mobilizes the cells that do the actual repair. Think of BPC-157 as building the roads and TB-500 as sending the construction crews.

WOLVERINE BLEND BENEFITS

The Wolverine Blend is used primarily for injury recovery and tissue repair. Here's what the combined peptide approach targets:

Accelerated Injury Recovery

Multi-pathway tissue repair means the body can address multiple healing bottlenecks simultaneously. Patients recovering from sports injuries, overuse injuries, and trauma often report noticeably faster resolution.

Tendon & Ligament Repair

Tendons and ligaments heal slowly because they have poor blood supply. BPC-157's angiogenic properties address this directly, while TB-500 promotes the cell migration needed for structural repair.

Muscle Healing

TB-500's actin upregulation is particularly relevant for muscle tissue repair. Combined with BPC-157's growth factor effects, muscle strains and tears may resolve faster than with rest alone.

Reduced Inflammation

Both peptides modulate the inflammatory response through different pathways. The combined anti-inflammatory effect can reduce pain and swelling while supporting — not suppressing — the body's repair process.

Gut Healing

BPC-157 was originally isolated from gastric juice and has a particular affinity for GI tissue. Patients with gut permeability issues, IBS, or GI inflammation may benefit from the BPC-157 component specifically.

Joint Health

Chronic joint issues involve multiple tissue types — cartilage, synovium, tendons, ligaments. The Wolverine Blend's multi-pathway approach can address several of these simultaneously.

Post-Surgical Recovery

Some providers use the Wolverine Blend as part of a post-surgical recovery protocol. Accelerated tissue repair and reduced inflammation can support faster return to function after orthopedic procedures. At Moonshot Medical, we coordinate peptide therapy with our physical rehabilitation program for a comprehensive recovery approach.

Convenience

One injection instead of two. One vial to store instead of two. Pre-mixed at the optimal ratio by a compounding pharmacy. For patients who would use both peptides anyway, the Wolverine Blend simplifies the protocol.

Important context: Most published research on BPC-157 and TB-500 is in animal models. The clinical evidence is strong enough that these peptides are widely used in optimization medicine, but the human trial data is limited. This is common across the peptide landscape — read more about why.

DOSING & ADMINISTRATION

Dosing is determined by your prescribing provider based on your specific situation. We do not publish a self-administration amount — the notes below are descriptive of supervised care, not a recommendation:

Dosing

Individualized by your prescribing provider and given as a subcutaneous injection. We do not publish a per-injection amount or schedule — your provider sets dose, frequency, and duration based on your situation and response.

Vial Duration

Each 3ml vial lasts approximately 6–10 days depending on your prescribed dose. Most patients go through 2–3 vials per month.

Cycle Length

Typical protocol runs 4–12 weeks depending on the injury and response. Some providers recommend cycling (e.g., 8 weeks on, 4 weeks off) while others run continuous protocols for chronic conditions.

Injection Site

Subcutaneous injection near the injury site when practical (e.g., near a knee or shoulder injury), or in the abdomen. Your provider will instruct you on the best location for your situation.

Storage & Handling

Ships overnight refrigerated. Store in the refrigerator (36–46°F). Once reconstituted, use within the timeframe specified by your pharmacy — typically 28 days.

Provider-determined: These are general parameters, not a self-dosing guide. Your prescribing provider will set your specific dose, frequency, and cycle length based on your injury, health status, and goals. Adjustments are made based on your response.

SIDE EFFECTS

The Wolverine Blend combines the side effect profiles of BPC-157 and TB-500. When sourced from a licensed pharmacy and used at proper doses, both peptides have favorable safety profiles.

Most common: Injection site irritation — redness, mild swelling, or itching at the injection site. This is typical with any subcutaneous injection and usually resolves within an hour.

Less common: Mild nausea, headache, or temporary fatigue. These tend to occur early in treatment and often resolve as the body adjusts. If persistent, dose adjustment usually addresses it.

Serious adverse events: No serious adverse events have been reported in the published research on either BPC-157 or TB-500 at therapeutic doses. The safety data comes primarily from animal studies, with human use data coming from clinical practice rather than formal trials.

The sourcing risk still applies. The side effects listed above are for pharmaceutical-grade peptides at proper doses. "Research chemical" peptides from unregulated sources carry additional risks — unknown purity, incorrect concentrations, contamination. If you're using peptides, the source matters as much as the compound. Read more about research chemical risks.

HOW TO GET THE WOLVERINE BLEND

The Wolverine Blend requires a prescription from a licensed medical provider. It's compounded by a 503A pharmacy — a licensed facility that creates custom medications for individual patients based on a provider's prescription.

You can't buy it over the counter, online, or without medical oversight. That's by design — peptide therapy should be prescribed, dosed, and monitored by someone who understands what they're doing.

Step 1: Consultation

Meet with a provider who works with peptides. Discuss your injury, goals, health history, and any contraindications. We run baseline blood panels to screen for contraindications and establish your starting point. This can be in-person or telehealth depending on the clinic.

Step 2: Prescription

If the Wolverine Blend is appropriate for you, your provider writes a prescription specifying the concentration, volume, and dosing instructions.

Step 3: Pharmacy Compounding

The prescription is sent to a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy. They mix the BPC-157 and TB-500 at the specified ratio, test for purity and potency, and ship it overnight refrigerated to your home.

Step 4: Administration & Monitoring

Self-administer subcutaneous injections per your provider's instructions. Follow up as scheduled to assess response and adjust the protocol if needed.
