Anyone can prescribe semaglutide from a video call. But a prescription without monitoring is just chemical weight loss with no guardrails.

The Telehealth GLP-1 Problem

Online clinics prescribe GLP-1 medications based on BMI and a questionnaire. They don't test your metabolic health beforehand. They don't track what kind of weight you're losing. They don't adjust based on body composition data.

The result: You lose weight on the scale—but studies show 30-40% of that can be muscle. You end up lighter but metabolically worse off.

We start with comprehensive blood work (60+ biomarkers) and a DEXA scan. We prescribe based on your full metabolic profile. We track body composition every 3-6 months to ensure you're losing fat, not muscle.

The result: You lose fat, preserve muscle, and actually improve your metabolic health—not just your scale number.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What are the common side effects of GLP-1 medications?

The most common side effects are GI-related: nausea, constipation, and decreased appetite (which is partly how they work). These are usually mild and improve as your body adjusts. Starting at low doses and titrating up gradually helps minimize side effects. Your clinician monitors and adjusts as needed.

Will I regain the weight if I stop the medication?

Studies show significant weight regain after stopping GLP-1 medications if nothing else changes. That's why our approach focuses on preserving muscle (via DEXA tracking), building sustainable habits, and addressing underlying metabolic issues. The goal is to eventually taper or discontinue with a strong metabolic foundation. Moonshot CrossFit operates in the same building at 542 Busse Hwy—many of our weight loss patients add structured training to maximize fat loss, preserve muscle, and build the habits that keep weight off after medication.

Am I a candidate for GLP-1 weight loss?

GLP-1 medications are appropriate for adults with a BMI of 30+ (or 27+ with weight-related health conditions). We evaluate each patient individually with comprehensive blood work and health history. Not everyone needs medication—sometimes optimizing hormones, thyroid, or metabolic function is the real fix.

Can I combine GLP-1 with hormone therapy?

Yes, and it's often beneficial. Optimizing testosterone or thyroid function alongside GLP-1 medication can improve muscle preservation and metabolic outcomes. We coordinate all treatments as a single integrated protocol based on your full lab picture.
