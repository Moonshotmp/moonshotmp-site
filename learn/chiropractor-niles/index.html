CHIROPRACTIC SERVICES FOR NILES PATIENTS

Chiropractic Adjustments

Targeted spinal and extremity adjustments guided by McKenzie Method assessment. Every adjustment has a specific mechanical purpose—restoring motion where it's restricted and reducing pain at its source. No cookie-cutter protocols.

Dry Needling

Thin filament needles target myofascial trigger points—those stubborn knots that refer pain to other areas. Effective for chronic muscle tension, headaches, and pain that hasn't responded to stretching or massage alone. Learn more.

Shockwave Therapy

Acoustic wave therapy that accelerates healing in tendons, fascia, and connective tissue. Strong evidence for plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow, and calcific tendinitis. Non-invasive with no downtime. Learn more.

Trigger Point Injections

For trigger points that don't resolve with dry needling or manual therapy, targeted injections can break the pain cycle. Performed in-office with immediate relief for many patients dealing with chronic myofascial pain.

Physical Rehab

Corrective exercise programming that addresses the root cause of your pain—not just the symptoms. Every patient gets a home exercise plan designed to build the stability and mobility needed to keep the problem from coming back.

IASTM

Instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization breaks up scar tissue and adhesions that restrict movement and cause pain. Particularly effective for post-surgical recovery, chronic tendinopathy, and fascial restrictions.

Sports Injuries

From weekend warriors to competitive athletes, musculoskeletal injuries respond to evidence-based principles: accurate diagnosis, targeted treatment, and progressive loading to get you back to training.

What is the McKenzie Method and why does it matter?

The McKenzie Method (Mechanical Diagnosis and Therapy) is an evidence-based assessment and treatment system for spinal and extremity pain. It classifies your pain by how it responds to specific movements, identifies the exercises that reduce your symptoms, and teaches you how to manage the problem independently. It's one of the most researched approaches in physical rehabilitation. Learn more about our chiropractic approach.
