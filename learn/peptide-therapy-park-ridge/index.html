Consultation & Medical Evaluation

Protocol Design

Based on your evaluation, we recommend specific peptides, dosing schedules, and cycle lengths. Recovery patients might get BPC-157 or the Wolverine Blend. Anti-aging patients might start with GHK-Cu or Sermorelin. Sexual health concerns get PT-141. We match the peptide to the problem — not the other way around.

Prescription & Injection Training

Your peptide is prescribed through a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy and shipped directly to you. At your visit, we teach proper reconstitution, storage, dosing, and subcutaneous injection technique. You leave confident in self-administration — no guesswork, no Reddit tutorials.

4

Follow-Up & Monitoring

We check in on progress, adjust dosing if needed, and monitor for any side effects. For patients on recovery peptides, we coordinate with training and rehab. For those on Sermorelin or combination protocols, we may track body composition changes via DEXA scan.

Are peptides legal in Illinois?

Yes. Peptides prescribed by a licensed medical provider and sourced from a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy are fully legal in Illinois. The FDA's 2023 actions targeted certain bulk compounding categories, but individual patient prescriptions through 503A pharmacies remain the legal standard. We do not use research-chemical or grey-market sources. Read our full peptide therapy guide for details on legality and sourcing.
