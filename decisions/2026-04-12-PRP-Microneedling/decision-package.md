## Decision: Should Moonshot Medical Add PRP Microneedling?

### Executive Summary

PRP microneedling is financially attractive (~$50 consumable COGS at $700-900/session = ~85% gross margins) and regulatorily straightforward in Illinois under Moonshot's existing MSO+PLLC structure. The cross-sell pipeline from GLP-1 patients (skin laxity) and existing optimization patients is a genuine competitive advantage that med spas lack. The Contrarian's strongest argument — brand dilution — is mitigated by Moonshot's medical positioning, which consumers perceive and value (validated by front-desk staff anecdote). The opportunity cost argument is invalid: current active projects (DEXA Upgrade, ScriptSure, patents) are not displaced by this addition.

### Recommendation

**PROCEED — content first, service second.**

Build SEO content authority now (zero capex, reinforces brand, captures search traffic). Launch the service when equipment/training are ready — low capex, fast break-even, doesn't violate 3 Active Project Rule since it's a service addition, not a system build.

Confidence: **HIGH** (4/5) — strong unit economics, clear regulatory path, validated demand signal from market and patient base.

### Contrarian Position

**The case against:** PRP microneedling is a commodity service with zero structural moat. Brand dilution from mixing optimization with aesthetics. Market oversaturated. Evidence base weaker than other Moonshot services.

**Rebuttal:**
- Brand dilution: Front-desk staff anecdote validates that consumers prefer the medical premium. Position as tissue optimization, not spa treatment.
- Zero moat: The moat is the practice context (existing patient relationships, near-zero CAC, content engine, diagnostic integration), not the procedure.
- Oversaturated: Saturated at the med spa tier, but thin at the medical-performance tier. Moonshot occupies a different lane.
- Opportunity cost: Invalid — current projects are not displaced.
- Evidence base: The market has validated PRP microneedling through widespread adoption and 78-83% patient satisfaction. A service can't be simultaneously ineffective and so popular it's commoditized.

**Unresolved concern:** Long-term brand trajectory if aesthetics becomes a dominant revenue driver vs. optimization services. Monitor the mix.
